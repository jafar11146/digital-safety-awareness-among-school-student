SMART HABITS

Digital Safety Tips

Small habits can make a big difference online.

🔐

Strong Passwords

Use long, unique passwords and never share them.

🔒

Privacy Settings

Check privacy settings and share personal information carefully.

🎣

Spot Phishing

Be careful with unexpected links and requests.

👤

Know Who You Chat With

Online profiles may not always be who they claim to be.

📱

Update Devices

Keep apps, browsers and operating systems updated.

🚨

Report Problems

Block/report harmful activity and tell a trusted adult.

WATCH OUT

Common Online Dangers

Knowing the risks helps you make safer choices.

🎣

Phishing

Fake messages or websites may try to trick you into sharing information.

💬

Cyberbullying

Harmful online behavior should be reported.

🕵️

Fake Profiles

Someone online may pretend to be another person.

💰

Online Scams

Be cautious of fake prizes and urgent requests.

REMEMBER THESE

5 Golden Rules

01Think before you click.
02Keep passwords private.
03Protect personal information.
04Be respectful online.
05Ask a trusted adult when something feels wrong.
